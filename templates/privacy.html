Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

Lesson Scheduler is a small web application for scheduling DJ and Producer lessons, built as a university software engineering project. This page explains what it collects and what happens to it.

What it collects

When you sign in with Google, Google provides your name and email address. That is the entire extent of what Google shares with this application — it does not receive access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google service.

If you use the application, it also stores what you enter into it:

  • Profile details you choose to add — a short bio, a profile picture, an intro audio clip
  • Lessons you post, if you are a teacher: date, time, capacity, skill level, location, lesson type
  • Lessons you sign up for, if you are a student
  • Messages you send to other users, and any attachments
  • Requests to change your role, and the explanation you give

Why it collects it

Only to make the application work. Lessons need a teacher and a schedule, messages need a sender and a recipient, and sign-ups need to be attributed to somebody. There is no analytics, advertising, tracking, or profiling of any kind.

Who can see it

Your name, profile picture, bio and audio clip are visible to other signed-in users, since the point of a profile is that people can see it. Messages are visible to you and the person you sent them to. Administrators can see the user list and role-change requests in order to approve them.

Where it is stored

Application data is held in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Heroku. Uploaded files — profile pictures, audio clips, message attachments — are stored in a private Amazon S3 bucket and served through time-limited signed links rather than public URLs.

What is not done with it

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. It is not used to train anything. There are no third-party trackers or advertising networks in this application.

Deleting your data

Email the address below and your account and everything attached to it will be removed. No reason required.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
